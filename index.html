The Three Starter Pokemon

Bulbasaur

A Grass/Poison type Pokemon. It carries a plant bulb on its back that grows as it evolves, drawing energy from sunlight through photosynthesis.

Charmander

A Fire type Pokemon. A flame burns at the tip of its tail, and it's said that Charmander will die if that flame goes out.
